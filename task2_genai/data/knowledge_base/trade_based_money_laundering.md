# trade_based_money_laundering

**Definition.** Moving value through trade: over/under-invoicing, phantom shipments, goods/payment mismatches, payments from unrelated third parties for trade.

**Key indicators**
- invoice prices far from market value
- goods inconsistent with the customer's business
- payments routed via third countries or unrelated payers
- round-tripping of trade proceeds

**How to distinguish.** Distinguish from high_risk_jurisdiction: TBML requires a trade/invoice anomaly; a normal trade flow to a risky country alone is jurisdiction risk.

**Typical action.** enhanced_due_diligence to obtain invoices/bills of lading; escalate_sar when mismatches are documented.
