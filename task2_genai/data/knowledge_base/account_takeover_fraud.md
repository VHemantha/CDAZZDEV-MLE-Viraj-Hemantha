# account_takeover_fraud

**Definition.** A compromised account is drained by a fraudster: new device/login, credential changes, then rapid outbound payments to new payees or mule accounts.

**Key indicators**
- new payees added and paid immediately
- atypical channel/device/location
- large transfers inconsistent with history
- customer complaint or recall request

**How to distinguish.** This is fraud against the customer rather than laundering BY the customer, though proceeds flow to mules.

**Typical action.** escalate_sar (and fraud team) on confirmed takeover; request_information/contact customer if unconfirmed.
