# rapid_movement_of_funds

**Definition.** Funds received and sent out within a very short time (hours to days) with little or no balance retained - a pass-through/layering pattern.

**Key indicators**
- in-and-out within 1-3 days
- outflows ~90-100% of inflows
- no economic purpose consistent with profile
- onward transfers to new or foreign beneficiaries

**How to distinguish.** Distinguish from funnel_account (many depositors -> one beneficiary) and legitimate treasury sweeps (documented payroll/supplier runs to known counterparties).

**Typical action.** escalate_sar if pass-through has no purpose; request_information if a sweep explanation is plausible but undocumented.
