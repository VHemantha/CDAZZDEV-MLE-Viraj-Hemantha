# round_tripping

**Definition.** Funds leave the customer (often offshore) and return disguised as new income, loans or investment, obscuring their origin.

**Key indicators**
- outbound to offshore entity then inbound from a related entity
- returning amounts close to outbound amounts
- loans or 'investments' from shells
- circular flows between related parties

**How to distinguish.** Distinguish from genuine intra-group treasury with documented loan agreements.

**Typical action.** escalate_sar when circularity with related shells is clear; enhanced_due_diligence otherwise.
