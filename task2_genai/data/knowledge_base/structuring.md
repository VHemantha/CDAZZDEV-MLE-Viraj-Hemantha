# structuring

**Definition.** Breaking cash deposits or withdrawals into amounts just below a reporting threshold (e.g. USD 10,000 CTR) to avoid reports.

**Key indicators**
- multiple cash deposits of 9,000-9,990 within days
- deposits split across branches/ATMs or across related accounts
- round amounts below threshold
- customer asks about reporting limits

**How to distinguish.** Distinguish from cash_intensive_business_anomaly: structuring is about SPLITTING below a threshold; a business whose total cash simply exceeds its profile is the other typology. Benign if amounts match documented business takings and are not clustered under the threshold.

**Typical action.** escalate_sar when clustering below threshold is clear; enhanced_due_diligence if borderline.
