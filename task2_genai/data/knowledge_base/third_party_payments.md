# third_party_payments

**Definition.** Payments made to or received from parties with no apparent relationship to the customer or its business purpose.

**Key indicators**
- payments to unrelated individuals/companies
- payer differs from contract counterparty
- no invoices or contracts
- unusual beneficiaries for the profile (e.g. medical practice paying foreign shell firms)

**How to distinguish.** Distinguish from legitimate supplier/payroll flows (documented relationships).

**Typical action.** request_information for the relationship; escalate_sar if no legitimate link exists.
