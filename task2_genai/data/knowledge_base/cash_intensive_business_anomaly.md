# cash_intensive_business_anomaly

**Definition.** Cash volumes inconsistent with the declared size or nature of a cash-intensive business (restaurants, car dealers, retail).

**Key indicators**
- cash deposits far above declared turnover
- sudden step-change without explanation
- cash inconsistent with sector norms or seasonality

**How to distinguish.** Distinguish from structuring (threshold avoidance) and from benign growth backed by financial statements.

**Typical action.** request_information (financials, POS reports) or enhanced_due_diligence; escalate_sar if co-mingling is evident.
