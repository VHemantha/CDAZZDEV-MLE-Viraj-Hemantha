# crypto_off_ramp

**Definition.** Conversion of crypto-asset proceeds to fiat through exchanges, OTC desks or P2P platforms, especially from unhosted wallets or mixers.

**Key indicators**
- inbound transfers from crypto exchanges/OTC desks
- immediate cash withdrawals or onward wires
- volumes inconsistent with declared income
- use of multiple exchanges or P2P counterparties

**How to distinguish.** Distinguish from a documented crypto trader with tax filings and exchange statements showing provenance.

**Typical action.** enhanced_due_diligence on source of crypto funds; escalate_sar if provenance is concealed.
