# high_risk_jurisdiction

**Definition.** Material flows to or from jurisdictions on FATF lists, under sanctions, or recognised secrecy havens, without clear business rationale.

**Key indicators**
- transfers to FATF black/grey-list or sanctioned countries
- secrecy jurisdictions / shell entities
- profile with no business reason for the corridor

**How to distinguish.** Jurisdiction alone is not suspicion: a documented trade corridor may be low risk. Escalate when combined with secrecy, shells or sanctions exposure.

**Typical action.** enhanced_due_diligence by default; escalate_sar on sanctions nexus or concealment.
