# funnel_account

**Definition.** An account receiving many deposits from multiple people/locations (often cash, often other cities) that is quickly drained to one or few beneficiaries, frequently cross-border.

**Key indicators**
- many small deposits from unrelated depositors or branches
- deposits in different geographic areas
- rapid aggregate withdrawal or wire to one beneficiary
- student/low-income profile receiving volume

**How to distinguish.** Distinguish from third_party_payments (paying on behalf of others) and from legitimate collections (rent, tuition pooling) with documentation.

**Typical action.** escalate_sar when multi-location deposits feed one outbound beneficiary.
